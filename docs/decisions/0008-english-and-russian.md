# 8. English and Russian only

November 2026 · Accepted

## Context

The guest agent was tested in four languages: English, Spanish, French and Russian. Claude speaks all of them without any work on my part, so "speaks four languages" says little. Ruki's guests write in English and Russian.

## Decision

Support and test English and Russian. Spanish and French scenarios are removed.

## Consequences

Fewer scenarios to maintain and cheaper eval runs, and the README makes a claim I can back up. Russian earns its place: it's where the best eval finding so far came from (see [9](0009-quantities-are-orders.md)). Item names have aliases, so творог and сметана match farmer's cheese and sour cream when I dictate a count.
